# Treasury Opportunity Discovery Catalog

## Purpose

This document consolidates Milestone 13B market discovery for the Treasury Intelligence Platform.

V1.0.0 proved that the platform can analyze individual opportunities, perform arbitrary position-size analysis, apply mandate-aware risk and eligibility rules, construct a portfolio, and produce an approval-ready €5 million treasury recommendation.

The remaining weakness is opportunity coverage.

The purpose of this catalog is therefore not to recommend investments directly. It records the broader opportunity universe discovered for a Slovenian d.o.o. managing EUR corporate treasury capital and identifies which opportunities deserve deeper research.

Discovery does not imply recommendation.

An opportunity may exist and be accessible while still being unsuitable for the model treasury mandate.

---

## Model Treasury Context

The reference treasury currently assumes:

- Slovenian d.o.o.

- EUR base currency

- €5,000,000 treasury capital

- €100,000 minimum useful allocation

- very high capital-preservation priority

- high liquidity requirement

- 100% immediate liquidity coverage target

- maximum T+2 settlement

- no meaningful unhedged FX exposure

- approximately 3% target yield

- target yield is a preference, not a hard constraint

- verified corporate accessibility required

- approximately monthly review frequency

The relevant question is:

> A Slovenian d.o.o. has €5 million of excess cash today. Given its capital-preservation, EUR/FX, liquidity and return requirements, how should that €5 million be allocated across the relevant accessible opportunity universe?

# 1. Home-Market Slovenian Opportunities

Slovenian opportunities deserve explicit treatment because the model company is a Slovenian legal entity.

Home-market access may reduce operational complexity and may provide access routes that do not exist for foreign issuers.

## 1.1 Republic of Slovenia Treasury Bills

### What they are

Short-term EUR-denominated debt issued by the Republic of Slovenia.

The Slovenian Treasury issues bills across maturities including approximately:

- 3 months

- 6 months

- 12 months

- 18 months

### What the treasury economically owns

A direct debt claim against the Republic of Slovenia.

### Return source

The investor purchases the bill below or around its maturity value and earns the implied yield as the security converges toward redemption.

### Access

Slovenian legal persons can participate through eligible primary-market channels.

Treasury bills may subsequently trade on the Ljubljana Stock Exchange.

This creates at least two conceptually distinct access routes:

    Slovenian Treasury Bill

            │

            ├── primary auction

            │   └── eligible Slovenian primary dealer

            │

            └── secondary market

                └── brokerage / Ljubljana Stock Exchange

### Current research focus

The scheduled 8 September 2026 auction includes:

- TZ233 — approximately 3-month bill

- SZ163 — approximately 6-month bill

- DZ125 — approximately 12-month bill

Actual auction yields should be captured after issuance rather than estimated in advance.

### Discovery priority

HIGH.

### Reason

Direct EUR sovereign exposure, home-market accessibility, short maturity and potentially attractive liquidity characteristics are highly compatible with the model mandate.

# 12. Discovery Screening Principles

## 12.1 Accessibility

Permanent V1 principle:

> No opportunity enters the actionable universe until both instrument economics and Slovenian-d.o.o. accessibility are sufficiently verified.

Technical availability is not sufficient.

Corporate legal access must be plausible and evidenced.

---

## 12.2 Position Size

A product is not attractive merely because it advertises a high yield.

Every promoted opportunity must eventually answer:

> What happens if this treasury allocates €X?

The final system supports arbitrary allocation sizes.

Diagnostic amounts such as:

- €100k

- €500k

- €1m

- €2m

- €5m

are testing fixtures, not permanent portfolio buckets.

---

## 12.3 Entry Capacity Is Not Exit Liquidity

Large TVL, AUM or issue size does not automatically prove immediate exit capacity.

The system must distinguish:

    ENTRY CAPACITY

            ≠

    EXIT LIQUIDITY

---

## 12.4 Headline Yield Is Not Realistic Return

The relevant return is approximately:

    headline / portfolio / market yield

    - product fees

    - access fees

    - brokerage commissions

    - bid/ask spread

    - slippage

    - gas

    - conversion costs

    - FX costs

    - operational costs

    - other defensible friction

    = realistic expected return

Risk remains separate from return.

---

## 12.5 Current Evidence

A product's existence does not prove its current economics.

    PRODUCT EXISTS

            ≠

    CURRENT RETURN VERIFIED

            ≠

    €5M IMPLEMENTATION ACCESSIBLE

            ≠

    RECOMMENDATION READY

Stale yield data should become an evidence gap rather than being silently substituted with unsupported assumptions.

## MEDIUM

## LOW / DIAGNOSTIC

Record but do not prioritize expensive research.

Examples:

- ICSLO / SBI TOP equity ETF

- smaller DeFi pools

- smaller RWA products

---

## SCREEN OUT

Examples include:

- products whose minimum investment exceeds available treasury capital

- meaningful unhedged USD exposure

- high-yield credit inconsistent with mandate

- locked products incompatible with liquidity requirements

- inaccessible corporate products

- tiny DeFi or RWA opportunities incapable of supporting useful allocation sizes

Screening out does not mean the product is universally bad.

It means it does not currently justify deeper research for this treasury mandate.

# 14. Strategy Versus Implementation

The expanded universe should distinguish economic strategy from investable implementation.

Example:

    BlackRock ICS Euro Liquidity Fund

            │

            ├── Core share class

            │   └── potentially accessible at €5m

            │

            ├── Select share class

            │   └── minimum may exceed treasury capital

            │

            └── Premier share class

                └── minimum far exceeds treasury capital

Similarly:

    EUR sovereign bills

            │

            ├── Germany

            │   ├── security A

            │   └── security B

            │

            ├── France

            │   ├── security A

            │   └── security B

            │

            └── European Union

                ├── security A

                └── security B

And:

    Morpho

            │

            ├── chain

            │   └── asset

            │       └── specific vault / market

This prevents inflated opportunity counts and keeps economic comparisons meaningful.
