# Milestone 13C.3 — Slovenian d.o.o. Accessibility Verification

## Purpose

Milestone 13C.3 verifies discovery-stage accessibility for the highest-priority

opportunities in the expanded treasury universe.

The question is:

> Can a Slovenian d.o.o. plausibly and legally reach a concrete investable

> implementation through a sufficiently evidenced access route?

This milestone does not determine:

- recommendation readiness,

- risk acceptability,

- realistic net return,

- position-size liquidity,

- execution quality,

- portfolio allocation.

Those remain responsibilities of the existing downstream V1 pipeline.

---

# 1. Accessibility Standard

For discovery-stage accessibility, the project does not require a live brokerage

account or an actual order ticket.

For an exchange-traded UCITS instrument, accessibility may be considered

sufficiently verified when all of the following are established:

1. the Slovenian corporate account jurisdiction is supported by the broker;

2. the relevant exchange or market is supported;

3. the exact investable implementation is identified and listed;

4. no known product-specific restriction blocks the corporate investor.

For direct fixed-income instruments, the project requires:

1. an exact security or sufficiently specific investable implementation;

2. a market or dealing route through which the security can be purchased;

3. a corporate brokerage or dealer route that can plausibly serve the

   Slovenian d.o.o.;

4. no known investor restriction blocking the company.

Direct subscription funds require stronger implementation-specific evidence.

Knowing that a fund exists is not enough.

The relevant subscription, distributor, platform or custody route must be

identified sufficiently for a Slovenian corporate investor.
