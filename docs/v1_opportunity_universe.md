# V1 Opportunity Universe

## Purpose

The Treasury Intelligence Platform V1 must answer:

> A Slovenian d.o.o. has EUR 5,000,000 of excess cash today. Given its capital-preservation, EUR/FX, liquidity, accessibility and return requirements, how should that capital be allocated across the relevant opportunity universe?

The V1 universe is not intended to contain every investable product globally.

It must instead contain a reasonably comprehensive set of serious alternatives that an EUR-based Slovenian corporate treasury should consider for liquid excess cash.

The universe must be broad enough that the final recommendation is not the result of cherry-picking a few instruments.

---

## Model-company constraints

Base currency:

EUR

Model treasury capital:

EUR 5,000,000

Minimum useful allocation:

approximately EUR 100,000

Target return:

approximately 3-5%, treated as a preference rather than a hard eligibility threshold

Primary objective:

capital preservation

Liquidity:

high, with immediate or near-immediate liquidity preferred

Settlement:

T+2 or faster

Unhedged FX:

0%

Corporate accessibility:

must be verified for a Slovenian d.o.o. before an opportunity becomes actionable

Review frequency:

approximately monthly

---

## Universe design principle

An opportunity may enter the research universe when its economics appear relevant to the treasury mandate.

It may enter the actionable universe only when both of the following are sufficiently verified:

1. Instrument economics and risk.

2. Slovenian-d.o.o. accessibility through a realistic access route.

Technical availability or public product existence alone does not establish corporate accessibility.

## V1 opportunity categories

### 1. Corporate cash and bank products

Purpose:

Represent the return available from leaving treasury capital within conventional corporate banking products.

Examples:

- operating account

- interest-bearing corporate account

- notice account

- short-term corporate deposit

- overnight sweep

Current representative evidence:

Addiko corporate deposits and the model-company cash baseline.

Role in portfolio:

Baseline and possible allocation destination.

Important distinction:

The platform must not assume that the entire corporate treasury is held in one bank account.

---

- UCITS status

- current net yield

### 8. EUR-denominated DeFi lending

Purpose:

Represent genuinely onchain lending opportunities with EUR economic exposure.

Initial representative:

Aave V3 EURC on Base.

Status:

Research eligible but not actionable.

Current blocker:

The complete Slovenian corporate operating route from corporate EUR banking through EURC acquisition, custody, protocol use, redemption and return to the corporate bank account remains unverified.

The category remains in V1 because it represents a materially different potential yield source.

It must not receive an allocation merely because protocol access is technically possible.

---

## Categories outside the V1 core universe

### Unhedged USD opportunities

Examples:

- US Treasury bills

- USD money-market funds

- USDC lending

- USD tokenized Treasury products

Default V1 treatment:

Excluded because the model mandate permits 0% unhedged FX exposure.

They may enter later only when a realistic EUR-hedged implementation is modeled, including the cost and operational complexity of the hedge.

### Longer-duration bonds

Examples:

- multi-year sovereign bonds

- conventional intermediate-duration corporate bond funds

Default V1 treatment:

Not part of the primary cash-management universe because their market-value sensitivity is materially less compatible with the model company's liquidity and capital-preservation requirements.

They may remain useful comparison opportunities.

### Equity, private credit and other risk assets

Default V1 treatment:

Excluded from the core treasury opportunity universe.

Their risk profile is inconsistent with the current model mandate.

## Horizontal-expansion rule

Do not create one analytics pipeline per instrument.

When another instrument creates demonstrated reuse, extract the smallest sensible product-type abstraction.

Examples:

- multiple ETFs may justify ETF position analysis

- multiple sovereign bills may justify sovereign-bill position analysis

- multiple money-market funds may justify MMF position analysis

- multiple lending markets may justify lending-market position analysis

Source adapters may remain provider- and instrument-specific.

Generic analytics should remain instrument-agnostic wherever practical.

---

## V1 completeness test

The opportunity universe is sufficient for V1 when:

1. The major relevant liquid EUR treasury categories are represented.

2. At least one serious accessible candidate exists in each included category where such access can be verified.

3. Material alternatives are not omitted merely because they require a different data source or access model.

4. Arbitrary allocation sizes can be evaluated.

5. Inaccessible or insufficiently evidenced products are explicitly rejected or marked as needing evidence.

6. The portfolio engine can compare the surviving opportunities and allocate the model company's EUR 5,000,000 without relying on hardcoded position sizes.

7. The final output explains both selected and rejected opportunities.

The objective is not maximum product count.

The objective is a defensible opportunity set from which a EUR 5,000,000 treasury allocation can be constructed.
